ABOUT WORLD SCOPE NEWS

Independent journalism. Deeper context.

WorldScope News is a digital publication covering Nigeria, Africa and the world. We aim to explain important developments through evidence, context and careful reporting.

OUR PURPOSE

Go beyond the headline

We examine what happened, what can be established, who is affected and what remains unknown.

OUR AUDIENCE

Global, with African perspective

Our coverage is international, while giving meaningful attention to Nigeria and the African continent.

OUR STANDARD

Evidence before virality

Social posts can alert us to a story; they do not prove the story. We seek primary records and independent corroboration.

Editorial standards

We distinguish confirmed facts from allegations, claims, opinion and analysis. We attribute sources, seek responses from people or organisations facing serious allegations where appropriate, protect vulnerable sources, correct errors transparently and label sponsored material.

We do not fabricate headlines, quotes, images, audience statistics or job listings. We do not present AI-generated imagery as documentary evidence.

Where a story is developing, we state what is known, what is not yet verified and when the report was updated.
