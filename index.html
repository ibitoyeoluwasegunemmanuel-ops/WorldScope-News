We don't fear the facts.
We follow the evidence.

We ask hard questions and investigate beyond the first statement. We distinguish confirmed facts from allegations and opinion, give subjects a fair opportunity to respond, protect sources where justified and correct mistakes transparently. Independence means accountability to evidence—not to power, popularity or pressure.

Our standards ↗
01

Go deeper

Trace claims to original documents, witnesses, data and on-record sources. Explain what is known and what remains uncertain.

02

Challenge power fairly

Scrutinise public decisions and influential institutions, seek responses and publish evidence—not unsupported accusations.

03

Own the record

Show sources, label analysis, timestamp updates and make meaningful corrections visible.
